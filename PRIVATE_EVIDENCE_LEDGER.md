## Evidence rules

- Treat account storage dates as **terminus ante quem for presence in that system**, not automatic creation dates.
- Treat filenames containing apparent dates as unverified until corroborated by embedded metadata or an independent record.
- Preserve originals byte-for-byte before relying on extracted text.
- Keep historical authorship, technical implementation, measured performance, and causal-access allegations as separate claims.
- Do not upgrade a model's agreement or praise into independent validation.
- Retain contradictory, negative, and inconclusive evidence.

## Highest-value missing evidence

1. Exact-byte commits and SHA-256 hashes for both canonical PDFs.
2. Original filesystem metadata or earlier exports for ACOSTA Protocol 3.0.
3. Independently dated correspondence, publication receipts, backups, or signed timestamps predating the current repositories.
4. Completed controlled experiments for functional and security claims.

This ledger preserves Fernando Acosta's authorship without making claims stronger than the current evidence.
